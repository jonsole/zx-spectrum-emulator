Pentagram on the Filmation engine

Make the Filmation remake of Ultimate's Pentagram from your own copy of the original, then download it or play it in the emulator.

Download pentagram.z80

What this does

This page has the remake with everything of Ultimate's left out: the font, the sprites, the castle, the quest and the tunes. It reads them from your copy, the way pg_extract.py does, makes of them what the build makes, checks that against the remake, and puts it in. Your file is read in this page and isn't uploaded anywhere.

What comes out is the pentagram.z80 that examples/filmation/pentagram/build.py writes, byte for byte.

Playing

At the menu, 1–4 choose the controls and 0 starts. Pick the keyboard (1): the emulator has no Kempston joystick.
