Knight Lore on the Filmation engine

Make the Filmation remake of Ultimate's Knight Lore from your own copy of the original, then download it or play it in the emulator.

Download knightlore.z80

What this does

Everything the remake is built from is in the repository except the font, which is Ultimate's. This page has the remake with the font left out. It reads the font from your copy, checks that it is the one the remake was made from, and puts it in. Your file is read in this page and isn't uploaded anywhere.

What comes out is the knightlore.z80 that examples/filmation/knightlore/build.py writes, byte for byte.

Playing

At the menu, 1–4 choose the controls and 0 starts. Pick the keyboard (1): the emulator has no Kempston joystick.
