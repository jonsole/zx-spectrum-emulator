Sprite sheet

Click a sprite to pick it; the left button toggles a pixel between ink and paper -- a clear one takes the picked colour -- the right rubs out to transparent, Alt+click picks a colour. The wheel scrolls, Ctrl and the wheel zoom, the middle button or Space and drag moves about. Keys: 1-4 the colours, P pencil, F fill, G grid, S the picked sprite, + and - zoom, [ and ] the sprite before and after. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo; Save writes it.
