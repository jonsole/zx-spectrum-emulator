Blocks

Open examples/stepscroll/world/: the blocks are in blocks.json, drawn with the tiles in tiles.json; map.json places them, and is updated when a block is renamed.

Pick a tile on the right, then click on the page to place it (its top left at the pointer); Escape or the right button stops placing. Click an entry to select it, drag it to move it, Delete to remove it; the arrow keys move it a byte or 2 lines. Entries snap to byte columns and odd bottom lines. Around the page, dimmed, are its neighbours on the map.
