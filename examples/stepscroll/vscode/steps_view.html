Step graphics

Open examples/stepscroll/world/: the graphics are in steps.json, and tiles.json, whose entries name them, is updated when a graphic is renamed.

In Chrome or Edge, Open world folder saves back into the folder. Elsewhere, Open files takes the JSON files and Save downloads what changed.

Click a pixel to toggle it; drag to paint more the same. On the left, in amber, is the shift-in strip: the 6 pixels that come in on the left as the graphic is shifted right 2, 4 and 6 pixels for the fine positions. Below, the 4 versions the build makes. Drag graphics in the list to rearrange them or move them to another group.
