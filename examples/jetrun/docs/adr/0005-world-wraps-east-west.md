# The world wraps east to west, and is bounded above and below

As in Cyberun, flying far enough east brings the astronaut back to where they
started: the map's columns form a ring. Vertically the world is bounded: open
sky above its top row of pages and solid rock below its bottom row, with no
wrap. Wrapping costs a little in the map lookups and in tracking objects
across the seam, and a world authored as a ring has to join seamlessly at
its seam.
